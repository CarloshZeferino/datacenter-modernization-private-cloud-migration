
# Operational Governance

## 1. Overview

Operational governance processes were implemented to
standardize infrastructure management, improve operational
reliability and maintain the security and availability of
the IT environment.

The governance practices covered both infrastructure sites.

## 2. Change Management

Infrastructure changes followed established operational
processes to improve traceability, reduce operational risks
and maintain service stability.

## 3. Operating System Patching

A patch management process was established covering:

- Operating system security updates.
- Operating system maintenance.
- Update planning and execution.
- Post-update validation.

## 4. Third-Party Software Patching

The governance process also included maintenance and
updates of third-party software.

## 5. Operational Standardization

Operational practices were implemented to improve
consistency across both infrastructure environments.

## 6. Scope

The governance practices covered:

- Site A infrastructure.
- Site B infrastructure.
- VMware virtualization environments.
- Operating systems.
- Third-party software.

## 7. Business Impact

The implementation of operational governance contributed
to improved infrastructure stability, operational
standardization and risk management.

---

**Note:** This document describes operational governance
practices. Specific approval workflows, maintenance

according to the implemented procedures.
